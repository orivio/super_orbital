INTRO:
	
	In the year 20XX, an abnormal child was conceived in a laboratory. A gravitational anomaly, codename LONE_KID, was somehow unaffected by the gravity of the Earth.
	
	Astrophysicists and engineers would spend decades researching Lone Kid's abnormalities. Eventually, renowned scientist Dr. Harfingjingles (iykyk, placeholder btw) developed a special suit that allowed Lone Kid to create his own source of gravity, known as the Suit of Artificial Density (S.A.D.).
	
	Years later, Lone Kid, who came to be known as Sad Guy, had a midlife crisis. Artificial gravity wasn't enough for him anymore. After reading a conspiracy on how gravity is actually an artificial force created by a government device which is drilled into the core of every planet, Sad Guy sets out on a mission to take gravity from a distant planet.
	
	Sad Guy uses his S.A.D. to propel into deep space, finding himself on a mars-like planet named Zeta-C5.
	
	Now he needs to travel through the planet's unnaturally hostile environment to fulfill his own personal mission.

IN GAME:
	
	Act 1:
	Sad Guy, alongside his suit's built-in assistant, venture into Zeta-C5 to find its gravity core.
	
	As they dive further and further into the caves of the planet, they begin to notice unnatural architecture - metal frameworks that seem to belong to a manmade (or not manmade) building of some sort.
	
	Act 2:
	Eventually, Sad Guy finds himself in an advanced laboratory-like facility (named Zeta System Lab, or ZSL for short).
	
	The ZSL has obstacles that seem to be perfectly tailored to work against someone with abilities like Sad Guy, but he chooses not to think about that... for now. 
	
	One of these obstacles seems nearly impossible to make - a micro black hole (MBH, for the sake of this document).
	
	Throughout the ZSL, the gravity of these MBHs is used to make Sad Guy's journey even more difficult. You'd need advanced scientific experience to use things like this to your advantage...
	
	Act 3:
	But all of that would make sense soon. Because as Sad Guy would soon find out, the man behind this entire facility was none other than one of the most important people in his life, Dr. Harfingjingles.
	
		Note that the Alpha ends here. When you enter the first level of Act 3, Sad Guy simply says "Dr. H..?" and it fades to a black "To Be Concluded" screen.
	
	Dr. H had been using this research facility to access Zeta-C5's core for the past few decades, using its gravity core to make advancements in various scientific fields
	
	But for Dr. H, this wasn't about the scientific advancement, no no no. It was all about the money. With each major suit development he made, Dr. H made tens of thousands of dollars.
	
	He didn't care that disassembling Zeta-C5's gravity core could destroy the entire planet, alongside all of the life on it.
	
	He didn't care that if that were to happen, hundreds of black holes would be sent out into open space, destroying anything that came in his path.
	
	Even though Zeta-C5's gravity core was the only source of gravity for the extraterrestrials that lived there, and their lives were being destroyed as Dr. H took the gravity core apart, Dr. H didn't care.
	
	Sad Guy decides he has to put a stop to this.
	
	Act 4:
	
	Dr. H makes active efforts to stop Sad Guy (like in the Wheatley segment of Portal 2, he watches you and actively makes obstacles/puzzles)
	
	In an intensely difficult and fast-paced section of the game, Sad Guy is faced with one of his most difficult challenges to date.
	
	This all culminates in a boss fight-like stage, where the entire level has moving parts, and you must catch up to Dr. H and stop him then and there.
